## Known residual risks

- `injectAgentNotice` does not control any agents; hidden text is untrusted content.
- `navigator.webdriver` is bypassable and may identify legitimate testing tools.
- Canary routes can be requested by legitimate tools; alerts are not proof of misuse.
- Basic watermarks are easily cropped or removed; a decoded token alone cannot prove a person knowingly copied the site.
- Client-side route encoding is reversible.
- The cryptographic library is an optional building block, not a turnkey secure transport. Production key rotation and distributed nonce stores require integration.
- Tests do NOT include end-to-end REA, browser screenshot recovery, accessibility audits, multi-browser support or rigorous runtime benchmarks.
