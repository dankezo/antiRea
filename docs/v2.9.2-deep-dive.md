# antiRea v2.9.2 - deep dive

> An audit release with fixes across new and older modules. The useful result is a reproducible account of defects, compatibility changes and rejected ideas. The local audit ran on Windows x64; seeded populations remain synthetic. Final counts, runtime versions and repeated benchmark samples are recorded in [audit-2.9.2.json](audit-2.9.2.json).

## Part 1 - What changed

Ownership accounting now handles out-of-order touches and bounded state explicitly. D2, seat occupancy and room guard bound open sessions and retained activity, and report saturation. Hold-type reports malformed sessions without non-finite features. Assertion comparison distinguishes all accepted JavaScript strings. Declaration imports validate their envelope and limits. Volume and EMP utilities reject invalid numeric input. Older surface-guard and e-value paths also received resource and hostile-input fixes.

No new research detector ships. The D2 law and RECOMB-03 were reconsidered with registrations written before fresh measurements; both proposed changes failed their audit gates. Zero runtime dependencies and host-controlled response policy are retained.

## Part 2 - Defects that ordinary examples missed

The baseline accepted different lone-surrogate tokens because encoding converted them into the same replacement bytes. Unescaped session identifiers could change a continuation URL's query or fragment. A volume identity named `__proto__` changed the result object's prototype instead of becoming an own property. EMP accepted negative or above-one probabilities, allowing meaningless curves and overflowing sums.

More consequentially, `maxSessions` was not a hard bound when every seat session remained open: a configuration of two still retained ten in the audit probe. Closed-session eviction alone could not stop identity minting; first-seen registries and activity histories also needed a bounded lifetime. Room-guard time iteration could stall when fractional tick arithmetic stopped advancing at a large epoch timestamp.

Surface traversal needed to account for cycles, sparse arrays, accessor properties and prototype-shaped keys. E-value resource rejection needed to be atomic: silently evicting or restarting an active process would change its statistical meaning.

## Part 3 - The repairs and their compatibility cost

- **Ownership:** bounded identities, per-identity events and total events; order-aware window accounting; explicit truncation diagnostics instead of claiming complete evidence after data admission fails. Unknown owners retain their denominator role.
- **D2, seat occupancy and room guard:** hard admission bounds preserve already-open sessions, summarize activity with sufficient bounded state, prune identity metadata and saturate counters safely. `saturated` makes incomplete telemetry visible and suppresses positive flags derived from that incomplete history. Future account creation is not counted as a new account. Safe tick construction avoids non-progressing loops.
- **Hold-type:** finite neutral numeric outputs carry `valid: false` and reasons for malformed or insufficient sessions. Hosts must check validity before treating a score as evidence.
- **402 and declaration:** assertion comparison uses exact UTF-16 code units under bounded token lengths; session URLs encode identity data correctly. Invalid options, unsupported import envelopes and oversized payloads are rejected. Declaration append refuses atomically at capacity; it never drops old entries to preserve the appearance of a complete chain.
- **Volume and EMP:** every identity, score and rarity field is validated; prototype-shaped identities remain own keys. Volume batches are capped at 100,000 cells. EMP requires finite probabilities in [0, 1], at most 10,000 grid points and 100,000 total samples. The helper has the same sample cap. Valid frozen thresholds and tie order are unchanged.
- **Older controls:** surface scans have bounded iterative traversal and explicit truncation; configured strip/block modes cannot claim success after an incomplete scan. Advisory remains the default. E-value steps and merges reject capacity or unsafe numeric growth before changing state, preserving active-process history.

These changes tighten behavior for malformed, oversized or incompletely observed inputs. Telemetry admission refusal does not authorize a new application sanction. See [LIMITATIONS](LIMITATIONS.md) and the [evidence summary](evidence/audit-2.9.2-summary.md) for the public diagnostics and accepted limits.

## Part 4 - Decisions that overruled attractive ideas

The D2 proposal tried to reduce lurker sensitivity with a different occupancy discriminator. Its preregistered independent experiment had 512 seeds per cell. Lurker flags fell from 53/512 to 3/512, but a minority-squat cell fell from 512/512 detections to 0/512. The candidate also failed its zero-new-FP gate. The correct action was DO-NOT-SHIP, not presenting the lower lurker count alone as progress. Frozen FAST and DRIP defaults remain unchanged; the missing original room generators still prevent an original-seed replay.

RECOMB-03 was judged anew as an independent pair experiment, explicitly without pretending to verify the absent C181 lineage files. Five preregistered replicates each scored 30,000 legitimate identities and 250 scrapers. Both pairs detected all 1,250 scrapers, and empirical thresholds reduced aggregate FP. Nevertheless, review-team FP under the empirical pair ranged from 16.32% to 63.92% on seasonal populations and 45.84% to 85.77% on overdispersed populations. Every replicate failed the new per-persona gate; one also exceeded the aggregate 10% gate at 11.49%. RECOMB-03 stays excluded because the benefit does not solve heavy-legitimate harm.

The research-bag review found no additional feature supported by complete fresh gates. A historical PASS, a VOID header experiment or JA4 corroboration shared by an office fleet is not sufficient reason to enlarge the runtime API.

## Part 5 - Evidence, not a single session's timing

The new-module tests include seeded model comparisons, malformed event sequences, exact-token mutations and input permutations. Hand-made mutants were compiled separately and injected only in isolated test workers, with unmutated controls. The volume/EMP area killed all 70 of its deliberate mutants; full per-module catalogs and remaining coverage limits belong in the [audit record](audit-2.9.2.json).

The room repairs were checked against the previous source on 80 seeds per module: 2,480 evaluations each for seat occupancy and room guard agreed apart from the added saturation diagnostic on unsaturated histories. Separate hostile probes exercise the fixed admission and tick-loop failures. Cross-runtime, package, range and browser results are recorded separately from publication status.

Performance uses repeated raw samples and machine metadata. Read the medians and method in [audit-2.9.2.json](audit-2.9.2.json); the old approximately-290-ms ownership figure and 36-ms surface figure are not carried forward as measurements of this machine. Browser demo checks are Playwright functionality tests, not REA attack-success or production bot-detection evidence.

## Part 6 - What remains unproved

Some historical generators are not in the bundle. D2 original-seed parity, complete RECOMB-10 population regeneration, full EMP population regeneration and the original RECOMB-03 lineage gates remain explicitly NOT VERIFIED. Independent fixtures test defined mechanics; they do not replace those missing populations.

The claims inventory distinguishes rerun results, historical claims and non-measurement numbers such as CSS or versions. A static resource inventory covers every source file but is not a proof of every allocation under every host callback. No production false-positive rate is inferred from synthetic cells. A hash chain still needs external anchoring to establish completeness, and every advisory signal still needs the host's authorization and response policy.

This release therefore has its own deep dive rather than a small closing note in 2.9.1: the changes span input contracts, resource behavior and older controls, while the two calibration proposals were measured and rejected instead of quietly shipped.
