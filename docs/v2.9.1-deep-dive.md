# antiRea v2.9.1 - deep dive

> Seven server-side primitives filled gaps between metering, evidence and host response. This account was completed during the 2.9.2 audit. It distinguishes port conformance actually rerun from historical lab outcomes whose generators were absent. No result below is a production detection rate.

## Part 1 - What shipped

The release added `createOwnershipProbe`, `createOccupancyEarlyMinimalSignal`, `createStepUp402Responder` with `manifestDocument` and `checkAssertion`, `createDeclarationLog` with `importDeclarationLog`, `applyVolumeRankRule`, `selectEmpMixThreshold`, and `computeHoldTypeFeatures` with `createHoldTypeScorer`. The signals are advisory; the host supplies authenticated identity and decides consequences. Calibration utilities are pure computations, not new authorization mechanisms.

This article records the 2.9.1 designs. The subsequent fixes have their own [2.9.2 deep dive](v2.9.2-deep-dive.md), because that audit also changed older modules. Final suite counts and repeated performance measurements are in the [audit record](audit-2.9.2.json), rather than copied from the old release announcement.

## Part 2 - The problems behind the additions

A record ceiling measures quantity, not whose records were touched. A silent-seat detector misses a squatter that emits a little activity. A bare challenge gives a cooperative machine no structured next action. Published preference declarations can disappear when a copy is republished, while a value watermark alone says nothing about when the declaration existed.

The remaining additions expose small calculations that previously lived inside lab runners. They deliberately do not fit a production population, assign trustworthy personas, or turn a synthetic separation result into an operating threshold.

## Part 3 - The mechanisms

- **Ownership:** counted touches and distinct owners in rolling windows, with public routes excluded and unknown owners contributing only to the denominator. It observes access patterns independently of authorization; a support queue can have the same pattern as a sweep.
- **Early-minimal occupancy:** the share of room capacity held by new accounts with one or two activities, all within the early interval. It complements zero-activity occupancy and reports a room state, never a list of guilty occupants.
- **402 shaping:** an HTTP response and manifest with pay, appeal and continue-free next steps. The host must already have triggered step-up. There is no settlement service, and an uncooperative caller can ignore the manifest.
- **Declaration log:** a host-maintained hash chain over preference declarations. Verification proves the supplied history's internal integrity, not crawler compliance, external timestamp attestation or legal recognition.
- **Volume rank:** rank all supplied cells by descending EB05 score; require at least two rare cells in the top 5%. Equal scores retain input order. The host computes scores and declares rarity.
- **EMP-MIX:** pool supplied FMR samples and select the largest qualifying grid point. Equal component counts recover the registered 50/50 composition; unequal counts are a different mixture, not independently validated by that registration.
- **Hold-type:** compute session-timing features and signed standardized scores against a host-supplied human reference. Feature separation alone is not a detector or a human-verification claim.

## Part 4 - What the audit actually reproduced

On Windows x64 with Node v24.19.0, the original RECOMB-04 algorithm reproduced its frozen JSON apart from timing metadata. The port agreed on 4,480 per-identity comparisons across the kappa fixtures. MID walker detection reproduced 0.90 / 0.85 / 0.85. The important correction is grouping: the lab's legitimate strata are generator-component labels, while SDK strata use observed volume. At kappa 1000 the original lab cell has one false flag among 147 MID identities; regrouping through the SDK puts that flag among 115 HIGH identities. The printed lab maximum 0.007 is not the SDK regrouped maximum 0.008696.

EMP arithmetic reproduced all 65 stored grid points, selecting 0.36 at every contamination level. Its full population generator could not be rerun because `r14-lab/common.cjs` is missing. The rounded-table example and raw JSON have different precision; this is not an arithmetic defect.

The original RECOMB-09 runner completed, and 1,000 SDK response shapes matched its extracted `shape402` byte-for-byte after JSON serialization. Its cooperative-caller outcomes reproduced 189/200 retreats and 188/200 assertions; this says nothing about callers that ignore the response. Hold-type reproduced 450 feature values and 270 scores on 90 stored sessions with zero numerical difference.

D2 matched its extracted causal series on 25 independent fixtures and 5,925 ticks. That does not reproduce the registered seed streams: missing room-generator imports leave their historical flag times and lurker peak NOT VERIFIED in this audit. The full RECOMB-10 population rerun is likewise unavailable without `harness-v2/index.cjs`. See the [audit evidence summary](evidence/audit-2.9.2-summary.md) for commands and missing inputs.

## Part 5 - Intentional mappings, not accidental parity claims

The declaration lab hashes a declaration block; the SDK hashes each entry's form and declaration. Their entry digests are not promised equal. The lab's ordering probe asks whether the last declaration precedes a copy; public SDK `predates` asks whether the earliest declaration is at or before a moment. Those agree on the original after-history vector but differ inside the history. Preserving the published API while documenting that distinction is more honest than changing either side to manufacture identical hashes.

The same discipline applies to volume strata and rounded FMR tables: per-identity rank conformance can hold while labels or printed precision differ. The audit records those mappings explicitly.

## Part 6 - Stated limits and the 2.9.2 follow-up

Unknown owners, public-route declarations, session identity and account creation times are host trust boundaries. Synthetic populations do not validate a production false-positive rate. D2 retains its weak lurker margin and its delayed-activity and extra-activity blind spots. A valid hash-chain prefix cannot prove that a tail was never removed; independent count and head-hash anchoring are needed for that claim.

The audit found resource, hostile-input and Unicode defects worth fixing across this release and older modules. They are described separately in [2.9.2](v2.9.2-deep-dive.md), with accepted limits in [LIMITATIONS](LIMITATIONS.md). Historical results that were not regenerated remain historical, not silently promoted to fresh audit evidence.
