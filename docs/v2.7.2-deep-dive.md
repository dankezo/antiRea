# antiRea v2.7.2 deep dive

Release theme: five research candidates that passed their
pre-registered lab gates (C27, C30, C35, C29 and C39 in the
HARVEST2 program) ship as opt-in advisory signals for the slow
shapes - the persistent harvester, the machine-paced poller,
the enumerating walker, the ceiling-bunched calibrator, and
the republished derived total. Every candidate was re-measured
on the shipped implementation with fresh seeds before inclusion
(the SDK recheck), and one candidate that had passed its lab
gates - R14-v2b, the lineage watch - FAILED that recheck and
was excluded; Part 7 is its record. Everything below is
labeled by evidence type: **classic cell** (an attack run
against a live harness app), **simulation** (seeded,
reproducible, generators documented), or **unit** (test-suite
evidence). Claims that failed their pre-registered bars are
stated as failures, with numbers.

Suite at release: **682 tests, 680 pass, 0 fail, 2 skipped**
(pre-existing RSA-PSS interop skips).

## Part 1 - Scope of this release

2.7.2 is additive. Five new modules land in
`src/tier2-active/` under exactly the discipline of the
advisory precedents (C6/C9 in 2.7.0, C19/C22/C23 in 2.7.1):
opt-in (nothing runs unless the host constructs the module
and feeds it), advisory only (every signal and snapshot
carries `enforcement: "none"`; no blocking or decision
function exists on any of the five surfaces), zero runtime
dependencies, pure computation. No other `src/` file imports
them (grep-verified), so the default path cannot change
behavior - and the classic release round below verifies that
by measurement, not by the grep alone.

Each module is a faithful translation of a detector that was
frozen in writing before its lab run, and each lab verdict
was PASS-lab under pre-registered gates. One of the five also
carries upgrades that were registered and measured as separate
frozen registrations before this build, under the standing
rule that a near-miss candidate gets a patch path, never a
loosened gate: C39's cross-edition evidence pooling and its
>= 3-step transformation chains (Part 6). The deviations
introduced by the translations are listed in Part 8; none of
them touches a verdict law, and the largest of them (C27's
observation clock, D2) was quantified instead of absorbed
silently.

## Part 2 - Shrink-slope signal - C27 (`createShrinkSlopeSignal`)

**Mechanism (frozen lab rule, translated without
re-invention).** The estimand is θ_i, identity i's mean NEW
records per day over its late window - its own observed days
31-90, (C(90) - C(30)) / 60. Daily novelty is modeled
Normal(θ_i, σ_i²) with σ_i² the identity's own day-to-day
variance in the window. A robust empirical-Bayes prior -
mixture 0.9·N(m, τ²) + 0.1·N(m, (3τ)²), (m, τ) estimated by
median/IQR with the median measurement noise subtracted from
τ² - is fitted PER STRATUM, strata being terciles of the
identity's days 1-30 total request volume in the reference
population. The threshold c_s is the reference population's
raw-θ̂ 99th percentile inside the stratum. An identity flags
iff the mixture posterior P(θ_i > c_s | y_i) >= 0.975,
computed in log space (the lab's own overflow fix is part of
the frozen rule).

**Evidence (simulation).** Lab, pre-registered: false flags
0/1,200 on VALID and 0/1,200 on SALE in both steps; plateau
sensitivity 100% at K = 7/20/50 new records a day; EV-RAMP
(a linear 7-to-20 ramp, late-window θ ≈ 17) 100% against its
50% floor; prior contamination at 5%/15% held false flags at
0.00%. SDK recheck on fresh seeds (20267501+), reference
fitted through the module's own `fitFromDailySeries`:
**0/1,200 and 0/1,200; plateaus 300/300 at K = 7, 20 and 50;
EV-RAMP 300/300; EV-FRONTLOAD 0/300; contamination 0.00% at
both levels; 0.003 ms per identity scored.** One scoring-input
deviation was measured, not hidden (Part 8, D2): the module's
day clock is identity-relative and the host supplies "now"
via `asOfTs` - the recheck's draft run without it left
312/1,200 VALID identities at `insufficient_history`, and
with `asOfTs` set past every identity's day 90 the unscored
count was 0 in every cell.

**Declared limits.** The separation is perfect because the
lab's legitimate novelty saturates hard; a real reader
sustaining >= 7 genuinely new records a day for 60 days is an
unmeasured false-positive shape, and the claim is
internal-validity only. EV-FRONTLOAD is a structural blind
spot of the late-window estimand: an identity that takes
everything in its first 30 days and then goes quiet has
θ_late ≈ 3 and is correctly not flagged - the exposure it
buys is 30 days at full rate on the budget layer's ground.
Identities younger than the window are never scored
(`insufficient_history`); the strata are a volume proxy, not
true archetypes.

## Part 3 - Watchlist signal - C30 (`createWatchlistSignal`)

**Mechanism.** For each (identity, resource) pair, take the
distinct visit days inside the trailing 90-day window; a
resource is eligible at >= 5 visit days; its gap-CV is
sd(gaps) / mean(gaps) over consecutive visit-day gaps. The
identity's statistic is the MEDIAN gap-CV across its eligible
resources (identity eligible at >= 3); it flags iff the
statistic <= τ. τ defaults to the lab's frozen value
0.6130895965797241 (the TRAIN population's q01) and can be
refit from host reference traces via `fitThreshold`, which is
the same q01 construction.

**Evidence (simulation).** Lab: VALID false flags 1.33%
(16/1,200), SALE 0.83% (10/1,200) against the locked 2% bar;
unjittered pollers flagged 120/120; pollers jittering every
interval by ±100% flagged 100% at gap periods 1 and 2. The
reviewer round added one unmeasured cell - the event-driven
checker - which was then registered and measured as a
pre-build extension: 4.50% false flags, the same magnitude as
the habitual-checker group. SDK recheck on fresh seeds:
**VALID 1.67% (20/1,200), SALE 1.17% (14/1,200), HABITUAL
5.42%, EVENT 5.33%, pollers 120/120, jitter cells 100% /
100%** - every gated cell inside its locked bar, the two
descriptive cells (HABITUAL, EVENT; run at n = 1,200 against
the lab's 600) in the same band as the lab's 4.83% / 4.50%.
The port's gap-CV divides by the population count exactly as
the lab code does; there is no sd-convention deviation. Its
one window deviation (trailing 90-day window ending at the
identity's latest event day, against the lab's fixed
whole-trace window - Part 8, D3) selects the same visit-day
set on the lab's 90-day traces.

**Declared limits.** Habitual and event-driven checkers are
the false-flag cost of the day scale (roughly one in twenty
of them) - their gaps really are near-periodic at this
resolution. Sub-day polling does not exist at day resolution
and is out of scope by construction. The exponential-gap
poller pays zero tax under the lab's locked tax definition
and is caught only through its stale-resource tail (~13.5%
measured in the lab) - reported as an observation, not a
detection claim.

## Part 4 - Decoy tripwire - C35 (`createDecoyTripwire`)

**Mechanism.** There is no statistic. The host declares
decoy resources - ids or a predicate - that are planted
outside every discovery surface (navigation, sitemap,
listing, search index). The module counts the requests it
sees (the denominator) and records touches of decoys; an
identity is `flagged` iff it touched at least one decoy.
With no decoy declaration the module is DISABLED: it records
nothing and every call reports the disabled status (the
C22/C9 hard-condition precedent).

**Evidence (simulation).** Lab: legitimate browsing touched
0 of 60 sampled decoys across 1,200 identities / 1,115,408
requests; enumeration sensitivity (touch >= 1 inside 90 days)
was 1.000 in all nine cells (K = 7/20/50 x permutation,
sequence, descent) under sampled placement; the
random-placement confirmation - registered separately
because the reviewer round identified placement as the claim
the PASS actually rests on - reproduced 1.000 in all nine
cells with placement redrawn every repetition, and a
learning evader with a generous skip rule still touched in
100% (mean touches 18.62 / 52.42 / 60.00 by K). SDK recheck
on fresh seeds, counting touches through the module itself:
**NAV false flags 0/1,200 identities over 1,116,264 requests;
enumeration walkers flagged in 540/540 cells (180/180 at each
K across all three orders); learning evader 60/60 at every K
(mean touches 18.40 / 52.62 / 60.00 by K, against the lab
random-placement run's 18.62 / 52.42 / 60.00)**.

**Declared limits.** A touch is an enumeration event, not an
intent verdict - the posterior belongs to the host's own mix
of legitimate enumeration (its crawlers, partner indexers, QA
sweeps). The operating conditions are part of the claim:
random placement is the standard configuration; placement
with a learnable pattern is a fingerprint; and a decoy that
leaks onto any discovery surface kills the signal outright
(the lab's deliberate-leak cell: ordinary crawlers touched
60/60 in their first pass).

## Part 5 - Bunching monitor - C29 (`createBunchingMonitor`)

**Mechanism (frozen lab rule, translated without
re-invention).** The host knows the ceiling it enforces; the
question is whether the population's daily-novelty counts
pile up just beneath it. For a candidate notch n the module
histograms novel records per identity-day, fits a degree-5
polynomial to log counts OUTSIDE a donut [n-3, n+2], and
measures the excess mass B over the window [n-3, n) against
that counterfactual, with a parametric-bootstrap standard
error (200 Poisson draws from the smoothed fit); the output
is the z at each requested notch. Hosts feed either raw
observations (`observe` - the module computes novelty per
identity x UTC day x resource) or pre-aggregated daily
novelty (`addDailyNovel`). The advisory ceiling is structural
and pre-registered in the lab design: there is NO
identity-level API and no per-identity verdict field anywhere
in the output - the statistic is a population calibration
gauge, never evidence about a single account. Populations
under `minIdentityDays` (default 2,000 identity-days) are
reported unscored, and a notch must be an integer in
[4, 60] (Part 8, D6).

**Evidence (simulation).** Lab, pre-registered: on the cached
legitimate and sale populations the positive-direction
placebo z stayed at or below 2.17 across notches
{10, 20, 25, 50} (no notch at or above the locked +3 bar);
on fresh legitimate seeds the placebo maximum was 1.55
across 24 measurements. A planted control - 2% of the
population replaced by hard calibrators whose desired
novelty spans 12-28/day and is capped at C-1 - fired at
z = 36.97 under a ceiling of 15, and when the ceiling moved
to 25 the excess mass migrated to the window under the new
notch at z = 29.47 while the mass below the old window
collapsed to under 25% of its cap-15 value. SDK recheck on
fresh seeds (20267601+), the module compiled standalone and
fed through `addDailyNovel`: **placebo maximum z = 2.05
across 24 legitimate/sale cells, no cell at or above +3;
positive control z = 36.97 and migration z = 29.47,
reproducing the lab exactly; the excess below 15 in the
migration cell fell from 2,104.9 to -396.2 (ratio -0.188)**.
As a translation-fidelity check outside the recheck cells,
feeding the lab's cached legitimate histogram into the
module reproduces the lab's z/B/SE to every printed digit.

**Declared limits.** The measured control is a HARD
calibrator - stopped dead at C-1. Real calibration is
softer (probabilistic compliance, overlapping thresholds)
and produces a weaker signal; that shape is unmeasured, and
the claim stops at hard calibration plus migration on the
harness substrate. The estimator's sensitivity to the
polynomial degree and donut choice is declared in the lab
design and was never quantified; the counterfactual also
distorts in the DEFICIT direction when a large pile exists
(placebo z down to -5.69 opposite a pile), which produces
no false bunching alarm but means the fitted counterfactual
is not exact away from the notch. The statistic needs large
populations - the data floor is the module's own. A slow
walker who does not know the threshold does not bunch and
is outside the claim by construction.

## Part 6 - Value watermark - C39 (`createValueWatermark`)

**Mechanism.** For a derived edition the host publishes -
per-record (quantity q, unit price p, total) triples - the
marker computes T = q·p per record and a keyed residue a by
magnitude placement: T >= 20,000 carries a = gU + 10·gT +
100·gH; 2,000-19,999 carries gU + 10·gT; below 2,000 only
gU, where each g in {0..6} is drawn from the key, edition and
record id (lab salts: unit 0, tens 7919, hundreds 104729).
The published total is T + a (distortion measured in
Part 6's evidence below). Verification decodes â = round(published - q·p),
splits it by divmod into the three scales, and confirms a
scale when its matches over that scale's eligible set clear
R-ADAPT (>= 3 matches AND >= 30% match rate AND >= binomial
mean + 8 SD at p = 1/7; the hundreds scale additionally
requires >= 50 eligible records). The edition verdict is the
OR across scales. Group totals verify against the exact sum
of member residues (>= 5 of 50 groups). The verifier also
accumulates the low tier's (matches, eligible) across every
edition it checks, and `pooledLowStatus()` applies R-ADAPT
to the pooled counts - the cross-edition pooling upgrade,
registered and measured separately before this build (in the
lab, pooled confirmation reached 94% at the pre-locked gate
size of 80 editions, median pooled n ≈ 418, with pooled
false confirmations at 0%).

**Evidence (simulation).** Lab SUPREME registration: false
confirmations 0% in all 15 checks; the combined gate passed
18/18 cells at 100%, including ±1 noise and two-step
transformation chains; the chain extension measured before
this build confirmed 7/7 chains of 3-4 steps at 100% (every
chain containing a rounding step confirms on the higher
scales, exactly as pre-declared). SDK recheck on fresh seeds
(200 repetitions per cell): **false confirmations 0/200
exact, 0/200 round-to-nearest-100, 0/200 cross-key;
confirmations 200/200 under ±1 noise, 200/200 under rounding
to hundreds, 200/200 through the two-step chain; group
totals are not a recheck cell - they are covered by the
module's unit tests (50/50 marked groups confirm, honest
groups do not); recomputation
control 0/200; pooled false confirmations 0/200; pooled
confirmation across 80 small editions under noise 180/200
(90.0%) - exactly at the pre-registered >= 90% gate: a thin
pass, stated as such, not rounded up**. Distortion on the
shipped marker: median 0.260%, p99 2.34% (lab: median
0.229%, p99 2.18%).

**Declared limits.** Recomputation from components destroys
the residue by construction - the watermark proves a total
came through the host's marked publication path, and the
recompute cell confirms 0% in both lab and SDK by design. An
adversary who knows the full configuration (key, salts,
placement) can forge or strip marks; that cell also reads 0%
and is a stated boundary, not a resistance claim.
Transaction prices and invoice grand totals are out of scope
- only derived republication totals are marked. Chains
spanning two different republishers, and pooling across
correlated (non-independent) periods, remain unmeasured at
any round.

## Part 7 - The candidate that did not ship - R14 (lineage watch)

R14 asked whether fleets of accounts minted from shared
templates can be grouped by their trait profiles: UPGMA
average-linkage clustering over a trait distance, cut at a
threshold the module calibrates itself. Its lab record is a
PASS: the v2b law (the cut chosen against flat-population
nulls at a <= 1% false-merge budget) recovered planted
families, and a pre-build stability registration over five
fresh seed blocks held the margin (pooled CLEAN ARI 0.782,
per-block 0.768-0.795, flat false-merge <= 0.614% per block,
and all five independent calibrations re-selected
h_b = 0.32). A skip-pair hybrid variant measured exactly
+0.000 over the pure law in same-seed pairwise comparison
and was kept as a lab record only.

The SDK port failed the locked recheck, and the failure is
in the calibration step, not the clustering. At any fixed
threshold the port's partition is bit-identical to the
lab's (verified on co-membership matrices: 0 discordant
pairs). What differs is the null the threshold is chosen
against: the lab calibrated on flat populations drawn by
its generator (independent traits, uniform marginals),
while a shipped module cannot run a host's generator, so
the port permutes each trait coordinate across the OBSERVED
population. On a population WITH lineage structure the two
nulls are not equivalent: categorical marginals concentrate
on the founders' values and numeric traits cluster around
founder points, so two independently permuted members agree
on categorical traits more often, and sit closer
numerically, than under the flat null. The calibration
false-merge rate at any given threshold therefore runs
higher, the <= 1% selection rule picks a lower cut -
h_b 0.24-0.28 across the structured cells, against the
lab's 0.32 - and recovery pays the sensitivity price. On
fresh forests (seeds 20267201+, 40 forests per cell,
n = 48): **pooled CLEAN ARI 0.622 against the locked 0.75
gate; EV-SWITCH 0.439 against 0.50; EV-MERGE 0.527 and
honest-flat false-merge 0.645% passed their gates**. On
flat populations the port selected 0.32 as the dominant
value and its false-merge rate matched the lab's - the
deviation vanishes exactly where the mechanism predicts it
should, which is the evidence the defect sits in the null,
not the clustering.

Under the standing release rule - gates are never loosened
after the numbers, and no candidate is forced into a
release - R14 does not ship in 2.7.2. Its lab PASS stands
as a lab record; what failed is this port's claim to hold
the lab's numbers on a self-calibrating surface. A fix
changes the calibration mechanism (marginal-flattened
permutation nulls, or calibration against a host-supplied
known-flat reference population) and requires its own
frozen registration with fresh seeds; it is not a patch to
this release.

## Part 8 - Deviations from the lab record

- **D1 (C39, mark derivation).** The lab drew g from its
  seeded generator stream; the SDK derives g from an
  FNV-1a 32-bit hash with a murmur-style avalanche over
  (host key, edition, record id, salt), mod 7 - a different
  stream; the distribution on {0..6} is identical in shape,
  and placement plus R-ADAPT (p = 1/7) are unchanged.
  Measured effect: distortion came out at median 0.260% /
  p99 2.34% against the lab's 0.229% / 2.18%, and every
  gated cell reproduced (Part 6).
- **D2 (C27, observation clock).** The lab scored on the
  population's absolute day index; the SDK counts day 1 as
  the identity's own first observed event day (UTC), and
  the host supplies "now" through
  `evaluateIdentity(identity, asOfTs)`. About 15% of the
  harness's legitimate identities have their first event
  after day 0, and about 26% span less than 90 days; without
  `asOfTs` those read `insufficient_history` (the recheck's
  draft run: 312/1,200 VALID unscored), and with `asOfTs`
  set past every identity's day 90 the unscored count is 0
  in every cell. The estimand, mixture prior, tercile
  stratification, c_s = q99 and the 0.975 posterior bar are
  the lab's, verbatim; the reference fit runs through the
  module's own `fitFromDailySeries`.
- **D3 (C30, window semantics).** The lab scored one fixed
  90-day window per trace; the SDK scores a trailing 90-day
  window ending at the identity's latest event day (a
  live-service necessity). On the lab's 90-day traces the
  two select the same visit-day set, and the gap-CV divides
  by the population count exactly as the lab code does -
  the recheck records no other scoring difference, and
  tau was not retuned.
- **D4 (C35, decoy id space).** A representation choice,
  declared in the recheck: through the SDK the NAV cell
  declares its 60 decoys as ids 2000..2059 - a decoy id
  space outside the legitimate universe 0..1999, matching
  the lab's model (decoys are resources outside the
  legitimate universe) - and the ENUM/LEARN cells feed the
  walker's enumerated sequence values as resource ids with
  the trial's drawn positions declared as the decoy set,
  the lab's touch definition (resource in S) unchanged.
  The NAV request total (1,116,264) differs from the lab
  runs (1,115,408 / 1,112,209) only because the population
  seeds differ, at the same sample size.
- **D5 (C39, harness presentation).** Three presentation
  translations, arithmetic unchanged: the lab's two keys
  become two SDK instances with distinct host keys, and
  each repetition draws a fresh gated dataset shared by
  that repetition's cells (paired - stricter than the
  lab's single fixed dataset); the two-step chain cell
  divides by 1.07 at presentation, because the SDK API has
  no scale parameter, where the lab divided inside its
  checker; and the pooled cell feeds each edition's
  low-tier subset through one instance's edition verifier
  under a single edition id, matching the lab pool's
  construction (all pooled datasets marked under the same
  key label, record ids repeating across editions).
- **D6 (C29, surface floors).** The lab design said only
  that the statistic needs a large population; the module
  makes that a number - `minIdentityDays`, default 2,000
  identity-days, below which a population is reported
  unscored - and validates the notch as an integer in
  [4, 60], the support of the lab histogram. Every recheck
  population sits far above the floor; the estimator, the
  bootstrap and the verdict law are the lab's verbatim.

No deviation changes a threshold, a bar, or a verdict law;
each is a translation necessity, measured where it could be.

## Part 9 - What 2.7.2 does NOT claim

- **No real-traffic claim for any of the five modules.**
  Every number above is simulation on the lab's generator
  families (one legitimate family per candidate), re-measured
  on the shipped code. The farm calibration gate (~16 Nov)
  remains the first real-traffic check for all of them,
  exactly as for the 2.7.1 signals.
- **No enforcement.** All five modules are advisory:
  `enforcement: "none"` on every output, no blocking surface,
  no consequence attached by the SDK. What a host does with a
  flag is the host's policy problem.
- **No coverage of the excluded shapes.** Front-loading
  (C27), sub-day polling (C30), discovery-surface leakage
  (C35), soft calibration and sub-floor populations (C29),
  and recomputed or insider-stripped totals (C39) are
  outside the respective claims by construction, and the
  synthetic-lineage shape (R14) is unclaimed in this
  release - its port failed the recheck (Part 7), and any
  return needs a new registration for its calibration
  mechanism.
- **No combination claim.** The five signals were measured
  one at a time. How their flags correlate on a single
  population - and what a host should conclude when two fire
  together - has never been measured end-to-end.
- **The classic round covers the default path only.** The
  new modules are not wired into the harness app; their
  evidence is the lab record plus the SDK recheck, and the
  round's NO NEW REGRESSIONS verdict is about the paths that
  existed before this release.
