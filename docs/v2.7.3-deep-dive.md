# antiRea v2.7.3 — deep dive

*The live-room release. One new advisory signal ships; one designed module does not, and this document leads with both facts.*

## Part 1 — What shipped, what did not

**Shipped:** `createHerdPhaseSignal`, an opt-in advisory detector for fleets driven by one shared scheduler, ported from research candidate H7-01 after two independent lab validations (Parts 3-4) - plus, in a second wave under the same release, five further advisory modules, one per registered live-room candidate, each built only after passing its own frozen lab pin (Part 6): `createSeatOccupancySignal`, `createActivityPhaseSignal`, `createVoteBrigadingSignal`, `createRoomHopSignal`, `createReconnectStormSignal`. Nothing on the default path changes: no existing module imports any of them, and the full pre-existing suite passes unmodified.

**Not shipped:** `createRoomGuard`, a room-pressure module (join velocity vs the room's own baseline, new-account concentration, post-join inactivity, churn) designed for this release and killed at its own pre-registered lab gate. Its combination caught the flood, drip, and churn cells and held zero false alarms on all four legitimate cells - and missed the seat-squatting cell completely (Part 4). Under the project's standing rule - no gate loosened after the numbers, no candidate forced in - it was excluded rather than shipped half-blind. There is **no** `createRoomGuard` in 2.7.3; any document or agent claiming otherwise is wrong. The occupancy leg it lacked was re-registered as its own candidate (CAND-273-1/2), passed its own frozen pin, and ships as the separate seat-occupancy signal (Part 6) - the combined guard itself remains unbuilt.

**Also shipped:** an agent-facing integration guide, [`docs/AGENT-GUIDE.md`](AGENT-GUIDE.md) - a decision tree from symptom to module, copy-paste wiring, safe defaults, a never-do list, and a verification checklist, written for the AI coding agents who increasingly do the wiring for small hosts.

## Part 2 — The case

The release was motivated by a live-room platform (watch-together cinema + karaoke rooms, 30-seat rooms, public brand "wetube ciné", site choigivui.com) whose operator reports rooms being crashed by what looks like a bot-driven DDoS. The platform's own 24 h online chart shows the whole online population dropping to zero in repeated clusters around 03:00-07:00 - the signature of a realtime application server dying and restarting under load, not of a pure network flood (a volumetric attack does not politely restart your app on a rhythm). The honest layering, stated in the case file and in every user-facing text of this release: **antiRea guards the room door (application layer: fake users joining, holding, cycling); the network door belongs to a CDN/firewall layer antiRea does not provide.** Case analysis: `CASE-LIVEROOM-WETUBE-2026-11-06.md` in the research tree.

## Part 3 — The H7-01 record (why herd-phase was eligible)

H7-01 (Kuramoto phase-lock) came from a cross-discipline harvest. Its Step 0 probe produced the design's central warning: per-identity phase-lock (R_i) separates a herd whose members share one grid offset from ordinary users **exactly as cleanly** as it separates a herd whose members each use a *different* offset - R_i measures grid-lock, not shared scheduling. Step 1 therefore registered a cohort statistic, R_cohort (the mean resultant of per-identity mean phases, unweighted), and froze the law before running: member iff R_i(900 s) >= 0.90; cohort confirmed iff members >= 10 **and** R_cohort >= 0.80. On fresh seeds (harness-v2 session starts):

| Cell | Scored | Members | R_cohort | Confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LEGIT | 82 | 0 | 0.0662 | no |
| HERD-SHARED | 86 | 86 | 0.9977 | **yes** (recall 1.000) |
| HERD-INDEP | 88 | 88 | 0.0794 | no |
| SALE (burst) | 96 | 0 | 0.0608 | no |
| VALID | 86 | 0 | 0.0419 | no |
| NAT-GROUP-DIRECT | 85 | 0 | 0.0634 | no |

All four binding gates passed (bootstrap separation overlap 0.0000 vs the 0.50 bar; false-positive member rate 0 on all three null cells against a 1% budget). HERD-INDEP is the cell that justifies the module's shape: without the cohort leg, the detector would flag 88/88 innocent-by-design identities.

## Part 4 — The roomguard pin (room-join adaptation + the RoomGuard kill)

Before building, both planned modules went through a fresh frozen registration (`roomguard-lab/REGISTRATION-ROOMGUARD.md`, seed family 20286xxx, grep-verified unused) on seeded **room-join** event streams: four legitimate cells - steady evening viewing; a **raid** (50 existing users joining within 2 minutes after an external link drop); a **scheduled start** (60 existing users joining within one minute at an announced showtime - legitimate phase concentration, the dangerous null); karaoke prime time - and six bot cells: instant flood (200 fresh accounts in 10 s), slow drip (1-2/min for an hour), churn cycling, seat squatting (30 fresh accounts taking all karaoke seats and holding silently), and a rejoin herd on shared vs independent 900 s grids.

Herd-phase, with the pin's scoping law (cohort = identities first seen within 24 h of evaluation), reproduced its H7-01 behaviour on join streams: shared-grid rejoin herd **confirmed** (60/60 members, R_cohort 0.9978), independent-grid herd **not confirmed** (60 members by R_i, R_cohort 0.0421), no confirmation and no scored scoped identities in any legitimate cell. Gates G-FP2, G-B5, G-B6 passed - the module was built.

The RoomGuard pressure combination passed its false-positive gate (never high in 4 × 20,161 evaluation ticks across the legitimate cells - including the raid at 25 joins/minute and the scheduled start at 60 joins in one minute, both held down by the newness and inactivity legs, since genuine raiders are existing accounts that proceed to watch and chat) and three of four bot gates (flood high at +60 s, drip at +31 min via the sustained path, churn at +30 s). **Gate G-B4 failed outright: in the squat cell the pressure never went high at any tick in seven days.** The mechanism was verified by a per-tick diagnostic on the same evaluator code, not inferred: the burst path's three legs peak at different times (velocity fires at +60 s while the squatters are still too young to enter the inactivity sample - inactivity fraction 0.14; by +180 s inactivity has matured to 0.82 but the 60-second join window has slid past the burst), and the sustained path's new-account fraction is diluted to 0.63-0.67 by genuine prime-time joiners, permanently under the frozen 0.70 bar. Squatting attacks **occupancy**, not join velocity; the registered signal family has no occupancy leg. Registration law forbade adding one after seeing the numbers, so RoomGuard was not built in any form - not even as descriptive telemetry, because the registration did not provide for that fallback.

## Part 5 — The shipped module's contract

`createHerdPhaseSignal(options?)` -> `{ observe, evaluate, snapshot }`. Feed one homogeneous event stream per identity (`observe({ identity, ts })`); `evaluate({ asOfTs?, cohortWindowMs? })` runs the frozen law: phase on the 900 s grid, identities scored at >= 8 events, members at R_i >= 0.90, confirmation at >= 10 members **and** R_cohort >= 0.80 (R_cohort over **all** scored identities' mean phases, unweighted - the lab definition, not a members-only average). Members are named only on confirmation; an unconfirmed result reports the member count and an empty list. Every output carries `enforcement: "none"`. State is bounded (identity and per-identity event caps with counters in the snapshot), evaluation is deterministic, malformed timestamps are structured no-ops, and the module has zero runtime dependencies and no wiring into the default path.

Declared limits (also in `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`): 900 s is the only validated scheduler period; legitimate groups with their own fixed machine schedules (personal cron fleets, office machines) were not in the lab populations, so the false-positive rate there is unmeasured - the cohort leg and the naming rule are the structural mitigation, not a measurement; an offset-randomising herd is invisible by design; identities under the 8-event floor are never scored, so one-shot floods are out of scope. All numbers are seeded simulation - internal validity only, not real-traffic performance.

## Part 6 — Wave 2: six candidates, six frozen pins, five modules built

The squat gap and five further live-room shapes were registered with cheap Step 0 proposals in `roomguard-lab/CANDIDATES-273.md` (research-bag INDEX, section 10). In the release's second wave, each was worked under its own frozen registration - law, gates, false-positive cells and kill condition written before any pin number existed - on the wave-1 simulator substrate plus the dangerous-null cell each candidate names (`roomguard-lab/cand273-lab/`: registrations, pins, `CAND273-RESULTS.md`; fresh seed family 20287xxx, grep-verified unused; every pin run twice, byte-identical). All six passed their frozen gates; five modules shipped (273-1 and 273-2 are two profiles of one module).

| Candidate | Frozen law (short form) | Decisive pin numbers |
|---|---|---|
| 273-1 seat occupancy (FAST) | New-and-idle seat share >= 0.50 sustained 600 s | Legit peaks: karaoke 0.0667, silent-audience 0.1333, raid 0.0200 - no flags in 20,161 ticks/cell. Burst squat flagged at +660 s (gate <= 900 s) |
| 273-2 seat drip (DRIP profile) | Same estimand, >= 0.34 sustained 3,600 s | Slow drip flagged 5,100 s before its last seat was taken (gate >= 1,800 s). Stress cell (all new users silent, 3x signups) peaked 0.2333, unflagged, descriptive |
| 273-3 activity-phase | Herd-phase law on activity timestamps, cohort scoped to first-seen <= 24 h | Shared chat herd confirmed (60 members, R_cohort 0.9230); offset herd not confirmed (0.0402); climax cell 0 members scoped AND unscoped. Unscoped shared herd does NOT confirm (0.2539) - scoping is part of the law |
| 273-4 vote brigading | 60 s windows, s = N - 0.5T, A = max(0, A+s), flag at A >= 12 over >= 2 windows | Legit A = 0 (incl. alternative-parameter population); raid mass-skip peaked A = 9 in ONE window - bigger than a brigade window's 7.5 - never flagged; brigade flagged before its third song |
| 273-5 room-hop | >= 16 distinct rooms in any trailing hour; self-disarm at > 25% flagged share | Surfers max 6/hour, heavy surfers max 11; mapper flagged 20/20 in its first hour; disarm never fired |
| 273-6 reconnect storm | >= 40 connects / 600 s AND median session < 5 s, per source; self-disarm at > 25% | Stable max 2, flaky 13, commuters 21 connects/10 min; storm flagged 20/20 in its first window. Distributed storm (200 x 25) flagged 0 - documented blind spot; no population gate registered, because legit commuter bursts reach 417/10 min at population level |

Design lineage, per the project's accumulated-knowledge rule: seat-occupancy is a population statistic in the bunching-monitor family (a room state, no identity verdicts); vote-brigading is CUSUM-style one-sided accumulation with the C27 lesson (slow shapes die by sustained accumulation, not speed) applied to windows; room-hop and reconnect-storm carry the C35 self-disarm pattern (a signal that can misfire or be poisoned at scale suspends itself and names nobody); activity-phase reuses the H7-01 herd-phase core outright rather than duplicating its math; reconnect-storm's per-source accounting follows the standing rule of never grouping by IP alone. Queue stuffing (a 273-4 variant) was registered but NOT measured and is not claimed anywhere. All numbers are seeded simulation - internal validity only, not real-traffic performance.

## Part 7 — Verification and coverage

Suite: **748 tests (746 pass, 0 fail, 2 pre-existing RSA-PSS skips)**, up from 682 in 2.7.2 - 66 new tests (12 herd-phase: shared-grid confirmation, independent-offset refusal, opposed-sub-herd cancellation, uniform-random null, min-members floor, event floor, 24 h scoping against an opposed old herd, asOf cutoff, malformed-event discipline, option validation, both memory caps, determinism; 48 across the five wave-2 modules pinning each frozen law on deterministic synthetic streams plus validation, bounds and disarm cases; 6 empty-instance regression tests added by the pre-release benchmark fix below), zero pre-existing expectations changed. `npm run check` (tests + audit script) passes; `npm run benchmark:build-sdk` passes. The default path is byte-identical except six added export lines in `src/index.ts` and the six new module files (verified by checksum diff over `src/` against the 2.7.2 tree), and no new module is imported by any existing module. The classic release round WAS re-run before release, as Part A of the pre-release benchmark, and matched the 2.7.2 baseline on every cell; Part B (a live-room integration benchmark over one continuous simulated day, 66,722 events) detected the squat, drip, join herd and vote brigade with zero false positives and caught one defect the unit suite had missed - `evaluate()` with no arguments threw on a fresh instance in seat-occupancy, room-hop and reconnect-storm (their default `asOfTs` fell back to an uninitialized `latestTs`); the fix makes an empty instance return the inert result, touches only those three new module files, and is pinned by the 6 regression tests above. The benchmark's two non-defect findings (self-disarm coverage of a concentrated night storm; activity-phase dilution on busy chat streams) are recorded in LIMITATIONS and the agent guide.

What the tests do **not** cover: real live-room traffic of any kind; scheduler periods other than 900 s; herds that randomise offsets (invisible by design); legitimate machine-scheduled subpopulations; queue stuffing (registered, unmeasured); brigading or squatting from aged accounts (newness is those signals' main leg); distributed low-rate reconnect storms (documented blind spot, Part 6); network-layer attacks (out of scope by layering). The farm calibration gate (~16 Nov) remains the first scheduled real-traffic check for the advisory tier as a whole.
