# antiRea v2.7.1 deep dive

Release theme: three research candidates that passed their
pre-registered lab gates (C19, C22, C23 in the HARVEST2 program)
ship as opt-in advisory signals, translated faithfully from
their frozen lab detectors. Everything below is labeled by
evidence type: **classic cell** (an attack run against a live
harness app), **simulation** (seeded, reproducible, generators
documented), or **unit** (test-suite evidence). Claims that
failed their pre-registered bars are stated as failures, with
numbers.

Suite at release: **621 tests, 619 pass, 0 fail, 2 skipped**
(pre-existing RSA-PSS interop skips).

## Part 1 - Scope of this release

2.7.1 is additive. Three new modules land in
`src/tier2-active/` under exactly the discipline of the two
advisory precedents (C6 in 2.7.0, C9 in 2.7.0): opt-in (nothing
runs unless the host constructs the module and feeds it),
advisory only (every signal and snapshot carries
`enforcement: "none"`; no blocking or decision function exists
on any of the three surfaces), zero runtime dependencies, pure
computation. No other `src/` file imports them (grep-verified),
so the default path cannot change behavior - and the classic
release round below verifies that by measurement, not by the

frozen in writing before its lab run (the lab Step 0 / design
documents), and each lab verdict was PASS-lab under
pre-registered gates - false-positive bars, teeth bars and
evader cells locked before any number existed. The deviations
introduced by the translation are listed in Part 5; none of
them touches a statistic, a threshold or a verdict law.

## Part 2 - Cohort box signal - C19 (`createCohortBoxSignal`)

### 2.1 Mechanism

The shape: a workforce that extracts on a rotating schedule -
shift crews whose pooled activity repeats as a box wave with a
period of hours to days (36 h and 48 h rotations are the
registered cells). Per-identity and per-session statistics
cannot see it; the signal lives in the cohort's pooled rate.

The detector is the lab's frozen Step 0 statistic, unchanged.
A host-declared cohort's served events are binned into
30-minute bins over a trailing 14-day window (L = 672 bins).
Box Least Squares (Kovacs, Zucker & Mazeh 2002) fits box
templates over a period grid of 6h-168h (x1.04 steps) with
fractional durations q in {0.03, 0.05, 0.08, 0.12, 0.18, 0.25,
0.35} and M = min(240, max(24, round(P/0.5))) phase bins; the
winning box's signal residue power S is then deflated by the
Pont et al. (2006) red-noise factor β, estimated from the
residual autocorrelation at the winning box scale (block count
clamped to n_b <= L/4), giving S_β = S/β. Significance comes
from 199 day-block bootstrap replicas of the same series - the
null preserves each day's internal shape and the circadian
rhythm while breaking multi-day periodicity - and a flag
requires p <= alpha (default 0.01). API: `observe(event)`,
`evaluateCohort(cohortId)`, `snapshot()`; failures are
`CohortBoxSignalError`.

### 2.2 Lab record

Pre-registered gates, run in order (simulation; the substrate
is the C15 lab's regenerated legitimate streams):

- **Gate 1 - regression on the folding candidate's corpse.**
  The earlier epoch-folding candidate (C15) died at a 9.80%
  false-flag rate on this exact legitimate family. C19's
  statistic: **0/150 = 0%** against a 1.5% bar.
- **Gate 2 - red-noise honesty.** β on legitimate cohorts:
  median **1.345** (p90 1.52, max 1.71) against a 2.5 bar - the
  deflation is real but not theatrical. Shift herds flagged
  5/9 at Step 0 (36 h: 3/3 with the period recovered at
  ~36.4 h; 48 h: 2/3; 24 h: 0/3, the pre-declared day-phase
  blind spot - see 2.3).
- **Gate 3 - the sale gate.** Pooled-rate false flags on the
  sale population: **0/50 = 0%** against a 2.0% bar. (The
  harness sale spike is a single day-5 episode, not a periodic
  box - the shape BLS + day-block bootstrap is built to
  ignore, and the contrast with the cohort-stack candidate
  that burned 80% on the same population is the point.)
- **Step 1 (detector frozen verbatim).** False flags **0% on
  all three families** (VALID-RAND 0/150, VALID-CHUNK-POOL
  0/50, SALE 0/50). Teeth: SHIFT-36 **9/9**, SHIFT-48 **8/9** -
  the single miss is the same β = 4.27 replicate that slipped
  at Step 0, cross-verified event-for-event. Evaders
  (desynchronized, ramped, continuous) all flagged **9/9** at
  ~zero measured cost to the evader - the detector reads
  pooled-rate shape, not member structure. Cost p99 **22.4 ms**
  per identity-checkpoint against a 50 ms bar.
- **Pre-declared exclusions, reproduced exactly:** the
  coverage-geometry herd (the parked C11 shape) scores **0/54**
  - C19 is not C11's reopening detector - and SHIFT-24 scores
  0/9 (2.3).

Unit evidence in the SDK (smaller scale, mechanism
assertions): three legitimate cohorts unflagged with S_β
6.0-7.1 (lab median 5.5, max 8.3 - same range); SALE unflagged;
SHIFT-36 and SHIFT-48 flagged with the winning period
recovered at ~36/48 h; SHIFT-24 unflagged, pinned by test as
the registered structural blind spot; a declared stratum never
flags while its statistics are still computed and reported;
evaluation is deterministic (two evaluations of the same data
return bit-identical p). Hand-measured cost ~175-225 ms per
24-member cohort evaluation, ~8-9 ms per identity-checkpoint.

### 2.3 Shipped form and hard conditions

Options: `windowDays` (14), `alpha` (0.01),
`bootstrapReplicas` (199), `declaredStrata`,
`minCohortEvents` (100), `maxCohorts`,
`maxEventsPerCohort`. Two hard conditions are code, not
documentation: a cohort in a host-declared stratum is assigned
status `"declared_stratum"` and can never be flagged (its
statistics are still computed - a host with a real 36/48 h
roster declares it instead of eating the flags); and the
statistic is cohort-pure - no per-identity score exists
anywhere on the surface, so nothing in this module can be
wired to a per-user consequence without another channel's
evidence.

The day-phase blind spot deserves its own paragraph because it
is structural, not parametric. The day-block bootstrap
resamples whole days, so a signal whose period is exactly 24 h
and whose phase is locked to the day is INSIDE the null
distribution by construction - no threshold change recovers
it, and none is offered. Hosts whose threat model includes
day-aligned rotations need a different channel; what this
module covers is the 6h-7d period band minus the day-aligned
line.

## Part 3 - Missing-mass signal - C22 (`createMissingMassSignal`)

### 3.1 Mechanism

The shape: the one-hit herd - many identities, each touching
the surface exactly once (one identity per IP, one page, gone).
Per-identity machinery is blind by definition: there is no
second event to accumulate. The statistic therefore lives on
the surface side, and it is the oldest estimator in the
program: Good-Turing missing mass.

For each (surface x window) cell, m = f1/n, where f1 counts
the identities that appear EXACTLY once in the cell and n is
the cell's total touches. A one-hit herd inflates f1 directly;
legitimate surfaces hold a low, stable singleton share. Two
scales: D1 (one day, primary) and D7 (one week, auxiliary -
recomputed from the 7-day totals, never an average of daily
ratios). A cell is scored only at or above the event floor
(`minCellEvents`, default 200; below-floor cells are counted
and reported as `below_floor`, never scored). Scoring is
one-sided, ties-inclusive conformal against a host-fitted
legitimate reference pool: p = (1 + #{ref >= m}) / (N + 1),
flagging at p <= `alphaDay` (0.02) on D1 and p <= `alphaWeek`
(0.05) on D7. API: `observe(event)`,
`fitSurfaceCalibration(surface, scale, cells)`,
`evaluateSurface(surface, scale)`, `snapshot()`; failures are
`MissingMassError`.

### 3.2 Lab record

Pre-registered gates (simulation, harness surfaces; the
binding surface UNION = resource ids 200..1999):

- **Step 0, overlap gate.** At the H = 200 one-hit
  identities/day design point, separation was total: D1
  legitimate p50/p99 = 0.0839/**0.1259** vs herd p05/p50 =
  **0.2238**/0.2554 - **overlap 0**; D7 legitimate p99 =
  0.00151 vs herd p05 = 0.1815 - **overlap 0** (D7 overlaps 0
  at every herd size; D1 still passes at H50 with overlap
  0.324 of the band). The DEEP and RARE surfaces taken singly
  also separate at 0.
- **Step 0, viral gate.** **0/168** viral-week cells flagged
  at D1, 0/24 at D7 - with the construction caveat carried
  into Part 6 (the lab's viral population is full-persona
  newcomers, not one-page drive-by strangers).
- **Step 1 (frozen detector, entirely new seeds).** False
  flags: VALID **1/280 = 0.36%** at D1 (D7: 2/40 = 5.0%,
  descriptive at that pool size), SALE **0/140**, second-week
  VIRAL **0/70**. Teeth: herds of 100, 200 and 400 one-hit
  identities/day flagged **100%** at both scales (H200:
  84/84 D1 + 12/12 D7); even H50 flagged 84.5% (D1) / 100%
  (D7). Evaders: EV-BURST (1,400 identities compressed into
  one day) flagged 3/3 on both its day and week cells;
  EV-REUSE-MIX (half one-hit, half 3-touch) still caught
  **39/42 = 92.9%**; EV-REUSE-2 (every identity reused >= 2
  times) flagged **0/42** - fully blind by construction (3.3).
  Cost p99 **0.248 ms** per cell against a 50 ms bar.

Unit evidence in the SDK: a pooled 40-cell legitimate day set
produces exactly **1 nominal flag** (2.50%, under the 3.0% bar
the lab locked in its Step 1 design - the flagged cell sits at
m = 0.1056 against a TRAIN maximum of 0.1025, p = 1/61 <= the
0.02 alpha: conformal behaving exactly at its nominal rate,
asserted as such instead of being tuned to zero); SALE 0/5
measured cells; the H200 herd flags at both scales; EV-REUSE-2
does not flag, with the mechanism asserted directly (f1 stands
still while n grows by 400); EV-REUSE-MIX and EV-BURST flag; a
below-floor cell is not scored even at m = 1; an uncalibrated
surface never flags; a late event is refused and the closed
cell is byte-identical afterwards.

### 3.3 Shipped form and hard conditions

Surfaces are the host's: `surfaceOf(resourceId)` maps a
resource to a surface name (the lab's id ranges are harness
artifacts and are NOT hard-coded). With no `surfaceOf`, the
module is DISABLED - the same hard-condition family as C9's
stratification law. Windows are anchored at `windowStartMs`
(default 0) and are not retroactive: an event belonging to a
closed window is refused `late_window` and never rewrites a
reported cell.

The reuse blind spot is the module's defining limit, and the
lab verdict stands on a clause that was registered BEFORE the
candidate ran: a herd disciplined enough to reuse every
identity >= 2 times hands itself to the channels that meter
reuse - the record union, input-space coverage (C6) and the
motif channel. That linked tax is a design argument, not a
measurement: no lab run has ever scored the combination
end-to-end, and this release does not claim it. A host that
deploys this signal ALONE owns the >= 2-touch shape in full.

## Part 4 - Lifecycle cohort signal - C23 (`createLifecycleCohortSignal`)

### 4.1 Mechanism

The shape: bust-out. Accounts are created, left dormant while
they age, then activated together in one synchronized burst -
the fraud pattern of aged-account bust-out and dormant-account
AML, transposed to extraction. The registered design decision
is to NOT hunt the aging phase (silent and indistinguishable
from legitimate dormancy); the gate sits at the burst and the
staging that precedes it.

A return point is an event preceded by an observed gap of at
least 7 days (`returnGapMs`). Each return point carries two
ratios against the identity's OWN history: ρ = gap / observed
age, and ν = (novel records in the 72 h after return) /
(pre-return novel records / max(1, pre-span / 72 h)) - an
identity with zero pre-return novelty has no defined ν; the
point is excluded and COUNTED (`returnPointsExcluded`), never
silently dropped. A member FIRES at a return point when the
conjunction holds: it touched the host-declared staging band
inside [rp, rp + 24 h), ν >= 4, and one dominant topic covers
>= 0.5 of its novel records inside [rp, rp + 72 h). The cohort
flags when at least `minFiringMembers` (default 6 - the lab's
locked 6/24) members fire on the same calendar day (the UTC
day of the first staging touch). API: `observe(event)`,
`evaluateIdentity(identity)`, `evaluateCohort(cohortId)`,
`snapshot()`; failures are `LifecycleCohortError`.

### 4.2 Lab record

The harness's stock legitimate generator cannot produce aged
returners at all (measured: 0 returns >= 7 days in 3,600
identities across 14/28/90-day horizons; the longest observed
silence is 6.02 days), so the returner and launch populations
were built by declared composition from the unmodified
generator's own phases - composition disclosed in the lab Step
0 document before any run, the same treatment the viral cell
received in C22.

- **Step 0, LAUNCH gate (cohort).** A legitimate launch
  pulling old users back en masse: **0/30 cohorts flagged**
  (kill line >= 2/30), with the staging ∧ synchrony conjunct
  firing on **720/720** return points - the gate is not
  vacuous; what legitimate launch traffic does not carry is
  the burst ∧ funnel half of the conjunction.
- **Step 0, identity gate (RETURNER).** Passed exactly AT the
  line: **12/600 = 2.000%** against a > 2% kill rule, and
  fragile per seed (3.0% / 0.5% / 2.5% - two of three seeds
  individually over the line). The ρ axis carries no separating
  power on returners (ρ ≈ 0.85-0.91 everywhere); the locked
  region is effectively the single ν >= 4 gate.
- **Step 1 (frozen detector, new seeds).** Cohort false
  flags: LAUNCH **0/60**, VALID **0/100**, SALE **0/50** -
  210 legitimate cohorts, not one member firing wrongly.
  Teeth: BUST-COHORT flagged **18/18** (bar 15/18), median
  firing members 24/24. Cost p99 **0.068 ms** per identity.
- **The identity re-check - a registered demotion.** The
  Step 1 confirmation re-measured identity false positives at
  **58/2,000 = 2.9% > the 2% bar**, triggering the consequence
  locked in the design BEFORE the run: the identity feature is
  descriptive only. It has full teeth (BUST-SOLO return points
  land in-region and fire descriptively, 300/300) - it died on
  false positives, not on blindness, and the PASS borrows
  nothing from it.
- **Evader measurements.** EV-DESYNC resolves by offset:
  1-3 days caught 36/51 (the SGI signature), **4-6 days fully
  blind 0/45**, 7-14 days blind at identity level but 50/120
  still firing through self-staging (4/9 cohorts flagged).
  EV-NOSTAGE (no injected staging) was expected blind and was
  NOT: flagged 8/9, because a hard burst touches the staging
  band by itself in ~37% of members (79/79 firings
  self-generated) - the staging conjunct leaks, in both
  directions (4.3). EV-SLOW is blind as registered (ν ≈ 1.5;
  budget/CUSUM ground).

Unit evidence in the SDK: the bust cohort flags with 24/24
members firing on the return day; a LAUNCH cohort with
synchronized morning staging touches fires 0/24 members and
does not flag; 5 firing members stay under the 6 threshold; a
solo bust return point lands in-region with its components
reported and NO flag field present (asserted by
`'flagged' in scores === false`); EV-DESYNC at a 5-day offset
fires 0/24, pinned; EV-SLOW at ν ≈ 1.6 does not fire; missing
declarations yield `undeclared` with each missing term null.

### 4.3 Shipped form and hard conditions

Two declaration laws are code: without a staging declaration
(`isStagingResource` or `stagingResourceIds`) AND a topic map
(`topicOf`), `evaluateCohort` returns status `"undeclared"`
and can never flag - the conjunction's terms are host
semantics, and the module refuses to invent them. And the
demotion is code: `evaluateIdentity` returns per-return-point
scores (ρ, ν, `inRegion`, the conjunction's components) and
there is NO `flagged` / `verdict` / `decision` field at
identity level anywhere in the module - a test asserts its
absence, so a future refactor cannot quietly re-arm the
feature the lab demoted.

The staging leak cuts both ways and hosts should read it
before wiring consequences: the channel also catches busts
that never staged (good), but a legitimate, heavy,
synchronized extraction team - a declared audit team doing
exactly burst-shaped work on a schedule - can pass through
this gate (the lab measured only a browse-normal launch cell
for that side). The mitigation is the existing one: declared
protected cohorts are a registry decision the host already
owns; this signal does not replace it.

## Part 5 - Deviations from the lab record (L1-L5)

Stated in the build report and repeated here so the deep dive
is self-contained. None of the five touches a statistic, a
threshold or a verdict law; every shipped default equals the
lab's locked number (C19: grid / q set / β / 199 replicas /
α 0.01; C22: floor 200, α 0.02 / 0.05; C23: 7-day gap, 24 h
staging window, 72 h burst window, ν >= 4, funnel >= 0.5,
synchrony 6).

- **L1 - C19 bootstrap seeding.** The lab drew each replica
  from an independent stream seeded by cohort identity. The
  SDK seeds ONE stream from an FNV-1a hash of the binned
  series itself: same null structure (day blocks drawn with
  replacement), different concrete draws, so p on identical
  data differs from the lab's in detail while the statistic,
  deflation and flag law are identical. The reason is a host
  property the lab did not need: evaluation is a pure,
  reproducible function of the retained data (determinism is
  pinned by test - two evaluations return bit-identical p).
- **L2 - C22 legitimate-cell bar in the SDK test.** The lab's
  own Step 1 design replaced the precedential 2.0% FP bar
  with 3.0% BEFORE running, because with the detector's
  nominal α = 2%, a 2.0% bar over 280 cells is a coin flip by
  construction. The SDK test asserts against the same 3.0%
  bar and asserts the exact nominal count (1/40). The
  detector's α is the lab's, unchanged.
- **L3 - Windows and time anchors.** The lab anchored
  everything to its population's t = 0. The SDK translates:
  C19 scores a trailing 14-day window ending at the cohort's
  latest event (the final instant clamped into the last bin);
  C22 anchors day/week cells at `windowStartMs`; C23's
  "calendar day" is the UTC day `floor(ts / 86,400,000)`
  instead of a population-relative day. Each module's header
  states its anchor.
- **L4 - C19 cohort size.** The lab locked N = 24. The SDK
  scores any host-declared cohort, gated only by
  `minCohortEvents` - cohort composition is a host declaration,
  which is the scope the build was given.
- **L5 - Timestamp discipline.** All three modules refuse a
  non-finite `ts` or |ts| > 8.64e15 as a no-op
  `{ status: "invalid_ts" }` BEFORE any state is touched (the
  F-21 precedent from the herd modules), and throw named
  errors for other malformed fields (the C9 precedent). The
  lab had no such cell - its data was always clean; this is
  SDK tree discipline, not a statistical deviation.

## Part 6 - What 2.7.1 does NOT claim

- No enforcement from any of the three signals: each carries
  `enforcement: "none"`, exposes no blocking function, and is
  not wired into any default path. The classic round proves
  the default path is UNCHANGED - it measures nothing about
  the new modules (they were not in the harness).
- No per-identity attribution from C19 (cohort-pure by
  construction), no day-aligned-rotation detection from C19
  (the 24 h box is inside its own bootstrap null, lab 0/9),
  and no coverage-geometry detection from C19 (lab 0/54 - the
  parked spectral watch stays parked).
- No β guarantee from C19: β can over-deflate and swallow a
  real signal when residuals stay correlated at box scale
  (the missed 48 h replicate, β = 4.27). A pooled-β variant
  is a new research registration, not a patch.
- No standalone coverage from C22 against reuse-disciplined
  herds (EV-REUSE-2, lab 0/42): the PASS rests on the
  pre-registered linked tax (union / C6 / motif channels),
  and that combination has never been measured end-to-end.
- No drive-by viral measurement anywhere in this release:
  the cell that matters for C22 (a stranger views exactly one
  deep page and leaves, pumping f1 on deep surfaces) exists
  in NO lab run on real host logs. Hosts with that traffic
  shape must measure it before treating a C22 flag as herd
  evidence.
- No identity-level verdict from C23: the identity feature is
  descriptive by measured demotion (re-check FP 2.9% > 2%),
  and reading its scores as flags re-creates that rate. No
  desync coverage at 4-6 day offsets (0/45) and no slow-burst
  coverage (budget/CUSUM ground) from C23 either.
- No clean staging semantics from C23: the staging conjunct
  leaks (~37% of bursting members self-touch the band), so a
  flag is evidence about burst ∧ funnel ∧ synchrony - and a
  legitimate synchronized heavy-extraction team can pass the
  same gate. The protected-cohort registry, not this
  statistic, is the mitigation.
- No real-traffic calibration for anything in this release.
  All three modules were validated on ONE simulated
  legitimate-generator family (plus declared compositions for
  populations that generator cannot produce); the lab-scale
  false-positive pools (280 cells, 210 cohorts, 2,000
  re-checked identities) are NOT reproduced at full size in
  the SDK tests, which port the decisive cells at smaller
  scale and assert pooled rates only where the sample size
  carries them (C22 VALID). The CUSUM farm fit remains
  calendar-gated (earliest honest fit ~2026-11-16, holdout
  verdict after), and nothing in 2.7.1 moves it.
