# antiRea v2.7.5 - deep dive

> The room guard that 2.7.3 excluded at its own gate, rebuilt with the leg it lacked - and the evidence layer that four neighbour dissections produced. Everything below is a lab record on seeded simulation or deterministic conformance; no number here is a real-traffic measurement.

## Part 1 - What shipped

Five new modules, all additive, all advisory (`enforcement: "none"` wherever a module has outputs about a room or an identity). Nothing on the default path changes: no existing module imports a new one, and all 758 pre-existing tests pass unmodified.

- `createRoomGuard` - the combined room-pressure guard, pressure v2 (RECOMB-06).
- `createConfigProvenance` / `configProvenanceHash` / `canonicalSerializeConfig` - CS-02, config provenance pins.
- `classifyWebBotAuthVerdict` / `classifyHostVerificationFact` - BS-01, verified-neutral verification facts.
- `issueStepUpProof` / `createStepUpProofVerifier` - CAP-01, single-use step-up proof tokens.
- `evidentiaryRoleOf` / `auditEvidencePack` / `decorateSignalResult` - KT-01, evidentiary roles.

Suite: 793 tests (791 pass, 0 fail, 2 pre-existing RSA-PSS skips), up from 758 in 2.7.4 - 35 new tests, zero pre-existing expectations changed. The new tests pin, among other things, one CS-02 digest taken from the lab probe's own results file, the BS-01 mapping for all 17 shipped failure reasons, the CAP-01 check order and nonce side-effect law, the KT-01 audit packs, and the room guard's four paths on hand-built streams.

## Part 2 - The room guard, and why it needed a new registration

The 2.7.3 changelog records the exclusion: a frozen combination of join velocity, new-account concentration and post-join inactivity passed its false-positive gate and three of four bot gates, and missed the seat-squat cell outright - pressure never went high at any tick, because squatting attacks occupancy sustained over time, and the combination had no occupancy leg. The project's rule is that a failed gate cannot be repaired inside the same registration by adding the missing leg after seeing the numbers.

RECOMB-06 is that repair done lawfully: a new registration (seed family 20296xxx, disjoint from the original 20286xxx), declaring the occupancy leg up front - the estimand and both sustain laws taken verbatim from the already-shipped `createSeatOccupancySignal` (CAND-273-1/273-2) - and one gate change declared before any number existed: the squat gate is 900 s, not the sketch's 600 s, because the statistic's physical floor is ~660-720 s of seat-fill plus sustain, a fact the CAND-273-1 pin had already measured. The results file still records the first high tick descriptively against 600 s: 690 s does not meet it, and says so.

## Part 3 - The RECOMB-06 record

Seeded simulation, 30 s evaluation ticks x 7 days (20,161 ticks per cell), the original legitimate background plus one new cell. Pressure v2 = the original three paths OR the occupancy leg.

- False positives: pressure never high in any of the five legitimate cells - steady cinema, a 50-user raid of existing accounts, a 60-user scheduled showtime, karaoke prime time, and KARAOKE-SILENT (60% of seat sessions, old and new, emitting no activity at all - the occupancy leg's most dangerous null). Legitimate occupancy peaked at 0.02-0.10 against the 0.34 DRIP threshold.
- FLOOD (200 fresh accounts in 10 s): high at +60 s (gate 120 s), via the original paths.
- DRIP (fresh accounts every 30-60 s for an hour): high at +1,950 s (gate 45 min), via the original paths; the occupancy leg fired later (+2,790 s FAST / +5,070 s DRIP), descriptively.
- CHURN (40 fresh accounts cycling 5 s dwells): high at +30 s (gate 15 min).
- SQUAT (30 fresh accounts take all karaoke seats in 120 s, silent, 1 h): high at +690 s (gate 900 s), via the occupancy FAST law only; the original three paths stayed silent at every tick, reproducing the 2.7.3 diagnosis on fresh seeds.
- SQUAT-DRIP (30 fresh accounts seat slowly, ~450 s apart): the occupancy DRIP law flagged 5,100 s before the last bot took its seat (gate: at least 1,800 s of warning).

Declared open, in the registration and in the module header: hole A1. A squatter who emits even 1-2 activity events leaves the occupancy leg's zero-activity definition, and the rate paths do not see a slow silent minority either. A1 needs a candidate about activity quality; this release does not pretend to close it, and a unit test pins the escape so it cannot be forgotten.

## Part 4 - The shipped module's contract

One instance per room; the host feeds `join` / `leave` / `activity` events (with `accountCreatedTs` when known) and reads `evaluate()`. Evaluation replays the lab's tick semantics exactly: windows are (t-W, t], newness is creation less than 24 h before the join (or first-seen), the velocity baseline is joins per minute over [t-24h, t-10min] floored at 0.5, and the occupancy laws are the shipped profiles (FAST 0.50 / 600 s, DRIP 0.34 / 3,600 s). The result separates history from state: `flagged` / `firstFlagTs` record that pressure has been high; `pressureHighNow` and `recommendStepUpForNewJoins` describe the latest tick; per-path first-fire times and the current component values are reported for the host's dashboard. There is no identity-level output and no API that names a member - the consequence surface is exactly "step up new joins while pressure is high", the pattern the agent guide wires through `createVerificationGrace`.

## Part 5 - The evidence layer (CS-02, BS-01, CAP-01, KT-01)

These four came out of the neighbour-dissection line (CrowdSec, bot-signal, Cap, Kitsune): each neighbour was read from its own sources, each transferable mechanism was registered as a candidate, and each candidate ran a frozen Step 0 in the lab before any build. All four Step 0s passed on 2026-11-09; the shipped ports are held to the lab artifacts by unit tests, not by resemblance.

- **CS-02 ProvenancePin.** A signal's verdict depends on its effective configuration; CS-02 pins it: SHA-256 over a canonical serialization of `{ kind, version, options }`, where options are the caller's values resolved under the factory's declared defaults (undeclared fields never enter). Lab probe over the 43 declared options of the 7 kinds shipped in 2.7.4: cross-process determinism 100%, default-equivalence 100%, mutation sensitivity 100%, 0 collisions, ~0.005 ms per pin against a 1 ms gate. The shipped implementation reproduces the probe's seat-occupancy digest exactly (pinned as a test vector), and `room-guard` joins the hashed kinds from this release.
- **BS-01 VerifiedNeutral.** Identity verification is not suspicion in either direction. The frozen table over the shipped Web Bot Auth outcome space (17 failure reasons extracted mechanically from the source, plus FCrDNS and published-range channels - 26 rows) assigns delta 0 to verified, no-claim and every unverified row, and "EVIDENCE" to exactly two spoof rows: a cryptographic signature failure (the one conclusive `invalid_signature` detail) and an FCrDNS contradiction. A replay verdict surfaces as a side event, not an identity fact. The classifier defaults any unknown reason or future detail string to unverified, so hosts never key enforcement on a detail string themselves. Facts optionally carry the CS-02 hash of the verifier config that produced them.
- **CAP-01 StepUpProofShape.** An Ed25519 token (three base64url parts) binding one identity, the CS-02 hash of the config whose step-up it answers, the hash of the host's challenge config, a single-use nonce, and a lifetime capped at 3,600 s with 60 s skew. Verification order is frozen (structure, key, signature, claims, temporal, binding, replay) and the consume hook - required at construction - fires exactly once per token that reaches the replay step, never earlier; the lab conformance ran 19/19 exact with the hook firing 4 times across the suite, and the unit tests re-run that law against the shipped port.
- **KT-01 EvidentiaryRoles.** Every channel gets a role under one graduation rule: CONVICTING only with its own passed gates, else CORROBORATING. The seven shipped signals were CONVICTING; `room-guard` joins them under the same rule via RECOMB-06. The BS-01 fact is the one conditional row (spoofed convicts, anything else corroborates). `auditEvidencePack` makes the consequence mechanical: unknown channel or mis-claimed role is invalid, and a pack with no fired CONVICTING item is ineligible - however many corroborating items fire. The tier restatement (observe / step-up / soft step-down with the 500 floor / tier 3 forbidden for inferred evidence) ships as data.

## Part 6 - What this release does not claim

- Every room-guard number is seeded simulation (internal validity), not real-traffic performance; the evidence-layer numbers are deterministic conformance over frozen tables, not field measurements.
- The pin conversion (C27+C35 from advisory to tiered consequences) is NOT in this release. It remains behind the farm gate of 2026-11-16 (CUSUM fit on two weeks of farm data plus holdout) and a production stratum-assignment mechanism; the evidence layer shipped here is its foundation, not its activation.
- Five dissection candidates have not run their Step 0 yet (CS-01, CS-03, AN-01, AN-02, AN-03) and are not in this release in any form.
- Hole A1 (Part 3) is open. A squatter who chats minimally escapes the occupancy leg by definition.
